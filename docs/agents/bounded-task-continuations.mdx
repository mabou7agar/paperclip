---
title: Bounded task continuations
---

Company alert-only monitoring continues to suppress general observation, repair,
watchdog and repeated blocker wakes. An explicitly owned background job may use
`set_task_monitor` with `kind: "task_continuation"` to read its result once without
changing the company's monitoring policy.

Include a stable job/candidate/stage identity in `externalRef`, a `serviceName`, a
`timeoutAt`, and a finite cumulative `maxAttempts`. Use the existing task, plan,
session, candidate and evidence. The native scheduler claims the check once and
uses its durable task-and-schedule wake key; repeated scheduler ticks do not
repeat it. Active executions, assignment, approval, quota, budget and independent
review guards continue to apply. The continuation is permission to read and
report the job's terminal result, not permission to repeat a proof or failed job.

Await the owned executor's terminal event when available. Otherwise schedule one
bounded result check after the expected finish. If work is still running, retain
the same job and report its state; never create a new execution or reset attempt
counters to obtain another check. Missing job identity, deadline or attempt bound
fails validation and cannot bypass alert-only monitoring. Existing
`external_service` monitors retain their prior behavior.
